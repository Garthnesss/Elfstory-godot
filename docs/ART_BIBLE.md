# ElfStory Reborn: Art Bible

From the decisions in PROJECT_TRUTH.md and `tools/ASSET_GATE.md`. Garth approves changes.

## Pillars
1. **Shape:** painted tabletop miniatures on walnut plinths, rim colour by faction (blue you, teal companion, red foe, gold boss, green townsfolk). Readable from the 45 degree diorama camera.
2. **Surface:** crisp, hand-painted, low noise. Textured TRELLIS originals for buildings and figures, not flat kits (Nov 21).
3. **Light:** golden hour above ground, one mood per branch below (crypt, barrow, mire, frost). AgX.

## Colour
60% ground and stone, quiet. 30% buildings, trees, props. 10% rims, telegraphs, loot, quest marks. What the player has not seen is dark; what they remember is cool and grey.

## Budget
Figures and buildings up to 3.5 MB and 50k triangles by Garth's exceptions, recorded per asset in `docs/asset_provenance.csv`.
